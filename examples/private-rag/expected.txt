[kb:double-charge] A customer was charged twice
A double charge usually comes from a retried checkout. Check
Payments on the invoice: if two payments share the same amount
and day, refund the second one. Pending duplicates drop off
within 3 days without a refund.

[kb:refund] Refund a payment
Open the invoice, choose Payments, then Refund next to the
payment. A full or partial refund goes back to the card used.
Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

[kb:export] Export invoices as CSV
Admins can export invoices from Reports, Export. Pick a date
range and the CSV format. Exports over 10000 rows are sent by
email.
